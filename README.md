## Overview

Tailoring businesses depend heavily on accurate information and timely order fulfillment. Traditional practices using paper notebooks and index cards are prone to misplaced records, physical decay, and delayed order tracking.

**TailorCraft** provides a centralized, secure digital solution that eliminates lost records and ensures seamless operations from fabric cutting to final release.

## Key Modules

- **Centralized Customer & Measurements Directory**: Full upper body and lower body anatomical measurements with bidirectional Inches / Centimeters conversion and multi-visit revision tracking.
- **Production Order Pipeline**: 6-stage Kanban board (*Pending, Cutting, Basting/Fitting, Finishing, Ready for Claim, Completed*) with rush priority alerts.
- **Financial Ledger & Claim Stubs**: Downpayment and balance tracking with dual-copy printable Master Job Order Tickets and Customer Claim Stubs.
- **Tailor Productivity Tools**: Fabric yardage calculator, customer SMS notification generator, and one-click JSON database backup/restore.
- **Academic Research Portal**: Evaluation metrics aligned with the ISO/IEC 25010 software quality standard with live survey feedback tools.
